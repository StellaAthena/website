Stella Biderman is a researcher specializing in large language models, AI interpretability, and open-source AI development. She is a co-founder of [EleutherAI](https://www.eleuther.ai/), a grassroots collective of researchers dedicated to open AI research, and a member of the [BigScience](https://bigscience.huggingface.co/) workshop.

Her research focuses on understanding and improving language models, with particular emphasis on evaluation, interpretability, and the societal impacts of AI systems. She is committed to making AI research more open, reproducible, and accessible to the broader scientific community.

Stella has contributed to a number of influential open-source language models and datasets, including the Pythia model suite, the Pile dataset, and GPT-NeoX. Her work has been recognized at top machine learning venues including ICML, NeurIPS, and ICLR.

Prior to her work in AI research, Stella studied mathematics. She is passionate about bridging the gap between theoretical understanding and practical applications in machine learning.
